# Paderborn (KAt) spike-origin probe — pre-registered hypotheses (2026-10-25, NOTES #8h)

Written and committed before any spike timing, current-channel alignment, filter or envelope result was computed.
Inspected beforehand: only the 10-23 sweep log (spike counts, headroom) and the .mat channel layout
(`phase_current_1`, `phase_current_2`, `vibration_1` share the 64 kHz `HostService` raster, 256,001 samples each,
so they are sample-aligned; `speed`, `torque`, `force` are 4 kHz; `temp_2_bearing_module` 1 Hz).

## Facts taken from the source paper (Lessmeier et al., PHME 2016, doi:10.36001/phme.2016.v3i1.1577)

- Test bearing 6203 (FAG; Table 1): 8 rolling elements, ball diameter d = 6.75 mm, pitch diameter D = 28.55 mm,
  contact angle 0°. Kinematic multipliers (inner ring rotating): BPFO = (Z/2)(1 − d/D) = 3.0543 × f_r,
  BPFI = (Z/2)(1 + d/D) = 4.9457 × f_r. (Schaeffler's 6203-2Z catalogue lists 3.0531 / 4.9469; the paper notes
  FAG/MTK/IBU 6203 geometries differ by 1–2 %, so every band below is ±3 %.) The paper's own Fig. 6 marks
  f_o = 76.25 Hz at f_n = 25 Hz (3.05 ×), consistent.
- Shaft speed: N15 = 1500 rpm (f_r = 25 Hz), N09 = 900 rpm (f_r = 15 Hz). Nominal values are used.
- Motor: PMSM, p = 4 pole pairs (electrical fundamental f_e = 4 f_r); inverter KEB Combivert, switching frequency 16 kHz.
- Currents: LEM CKSR 15-NP, 25 kHz low-pass, 64 kHz. Vibration: PCB 336C04 accelerometer + Kistler 5015A charge
  amplifier with a 30 kHz low-pass, sampled synchronously at 64 kHz.

## Spike set (fixed)

Spikes = `paderborn.isolated_spikes` (frac = 0.5): samples whose step in and step out both exceed 0.5 × record peak
with opposite signs — the same definition as the 10-23 sweep. Analysed records: the 64 kHz records with ≥ 10 spikes
("spiky", expected K001 × 4 and KI04 × 4 = 8). KA04 (0–2 spikes) is the control for H3 only.

## Statistic for periodicity

Vector strength R(f) = |mean_k exp(2πi f t_k)| over spike times t_k = n_k / 64,000 s. Band statistic: max R over a
frequency grid of 0.01 Hz step (≤ 0.025 · 1/T) across the band. Band p-value (Rayleigh with Bonferroni over
M = max(1, ⌈BW · T⌉) independent frequencies, T = 4 s):  p = min(1, M · exp(−N R²_max)).
Significance level α = 1e-3 per band.

Decoy bands (not kinematic, not shaft harmonics): 1.37, 3.71, 5.53 × f_r, each ±3 %. A record's periodicity
result counts only if no decoy band is significant; otherwise the record is "decoy-contaminated" (clustered
spike times make any low frequency look coherent) and counts as no evidence either way.

## Hypotheses and falsification criteria

**H1 — electrical (inverter switching / EMI on the vibration channel).** Evidence in a record if either:
- H1a: the 16 kHz ± 1 % band (15,840–16,160 Hz; this band also contains the aliases of the 48 kHz harmonic) is
  significant (p < 1e-3), or
- H1b: ≥ 50 % of spikes coincide with a current transient, and that fraction is ≥ 3 × the null fraction. Current
  transient at spike n: max over k ∈ [n−2, n+2] of |Δi_k| (either phase current) above that channel's 99th
  percentile of |Δi| over the record. Null fraction: same test at 2,000 random sample indices (seed 20261025,
  numpy default_rng), excluding indices within ±2 of a spike.
- **Held** if evidence in ≥ 6 of 8 spiky records and H2 evidence in ≤ 2 of 8. **Falsified** if evidence in ≤ 2 of 8.
  Otherwise inconclusive.

**H2 — mechanical (bearing kinematics or shaft angle).** Evidence in a record if f_r, BPFO or BPFI (each ±3 %)
is significant (p < 1e-3) and the record is not decoy-contaminated. Since K001 is healthy, the bearing lines are
only expected on KI04 (BPFI); shaft-synchronous spikes (f_r) would point to a rotating source (rotor, coupling,
shaft-angle-locked noise).
- **Held** if evidence in ≥ 6 of 8 spiky records and H1 evidence in ≤ 2 of 8. **Falsified** if evidence in ≤ 2 of 8.
  Otherwise inconclusive.

If both H1 and H2 are falsified the origin stays **unexplained**; this probe then makes no mechanism claim beyond
the descriptive shape statistics below.

Descriptive only (no verdict weight): R at f_e = 4 f_r and 6 f_e (ambiguous: 4 f_r is also a shaft harmonic);
spike polarity split; spike height / neighbour level; same-sample `isolated_spikes` on the current channels.
Physical note recorded in advance: a one-sample excursion that returns in one sample has energy up to the Nyquist
frequency (32 kHz); behind a 30 kHz low-pass charge amplifier a genuine accelerometer signal should not produce
it at full height, so a pure-mechanical origin would also require the spike to enter after that filter or the
filter to be gentler than stated. This is an argument, not a test.

**H3 — a pre-fold filter restores headroom without removing fault content.** Filters applied to the recorded
signal before the (software) fold:
- LP10 / LP5: 4th-order Butterworth low-pass, f_c = 10 kHz / 5 kHz, **causal** (`scipy.signal.sosfilt`), as an
  analog anti-alias stage would be.
- MED3: 3-point running median. A real analog filter cannot be a median; MED3 only models a digital
  spike reject, which would sit after a conventional (non-folding) ADC and so is not available in front of a
  modulo ADC. Reported to bound what spike removal alone could buy.

Headroom = 20 log10(peak / max|Δx|) of the filtered record. Fault-line preservation: envelope spectrum
(4th-order Butterworth 500 Hz high-pass, zero-phase, analysis only; Hilbert envelope, mean removed, rfft over the
4 s record); line SNR = 20 log10(max amplitude within target ±3 % / median amplitude in target ± 20 Hz excluding
the ±3 % band). Target BPFI on KI04, BPFO on KA04 (control). Preserved if filtered line SNR ≥ raw line SNR − 3 dB.
Records whose raw line SNR < 6 dB are "untestable" for preservation.
Kurtosis before/after is reported but not used as a criterion: the spikes themselves inflate kurtosis (K001,
healthy, reads 13–20), so a falling kurtosis does not by itself mean fault content was lost.

For each filter: **held** if headroom ≥ 3.0 dB (the KA04 spike-free level) on ≥ 7 of 8 spiky records, fault line
preserved on every testable KI04 and KA04 record, and the full fold chain (`realdata.eval_record`, 6 ratios) on the
filtered spiky records gives 0 silent failures and the slew rule agrees in every cell. **Falsified** if headroom
≥ 3.0 dB on ≤ 4 of 8 spiky records, or any testable fault line is not preserved, or any silent failure.
Otherwise inconclusive. H3 (overall) is held if LP10 or LP5 is held (MED3 does not count — not realisable in
front of a fold). Expected in advance: low-pass raises headroom almost by construction (max step ≤ 2π f_c/f_s ×
amplitude for band-limited content), so the informative part of H3 is fault-line preservation and whether the
spike is removed rather than just smeared.

Seeds/parameters are fixed above. CPU only; no GPU.
